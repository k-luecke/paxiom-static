J-300 · R&D substrate archive

Upstream proof substrates (parked behind Receipts).

During April–May 2026, Paxiom prototyped five Ethereum and cross-chain attestation services (A-201 storage-slot proofs, A-202 sync-committee header verification, A-203 cross-chain message verification, A-204 transaction simulation, and A-205 historical MPT state). Building those envelopes revealed a simpler, universal gap across every x402 API: buyers lacked a standard signed receipt proving what any paid HTTP call actually delivered. That envelope became Paxiom Receipts.

J-400 · Boundaries & non-claims

What is open — and what isn't claimed.

Next Engineering Extensions
  • Additional EVM and Solana USDC settlement adapters beyond Base Mainnet & Base Sepolia in POST /v1/receipt/verify-live
  • Optional facilitator receipt cross-checking alongside direct on-chain Transfer log verification
  • Evidence-hash adapters binding upstream cryptographic witnesses (A-201/A-202) into payload.evidence
  • Hosted usage dashboard for monthly B2B X-API-Key accounts
Explicit Non-Claims
  • POST /v1/receipt/verify is strictly offline: it never claims on-chain finality and always surfaces unverified tx hashes as settlement_claimed_not_checked
  • POST /v1/receipt/verify-live verifies Base / Base Sepolia USDC Transfer events and counter-signs with Paxiom's published Ed25519 key; it does not retrieve off-chain Arweave archives in v0
  • No custody of customer funds — Paxiom never holds buyer wallets or escrowed balances
  • No third-party security audit certification or regulated legal attestation is claimed in v0
  • Upstream R&D services (A-201–A-205) remain experimental substrates, not active commercial SLA endpoints
